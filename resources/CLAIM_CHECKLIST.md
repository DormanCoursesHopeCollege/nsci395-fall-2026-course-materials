# Claim checklist

Before writing a result claim, answer:

1. What exact comparison or pattern did the analysis measure?
2. Is the result descriptive, predictive, or mechanistic?
3. Was performance evaluated on held-out data?
4. Does the method use labels, or is the label relationship observed afterward?
5. What alternative explanation or nuisance variable remains?
6. Which words would overstate the evidence?

## Useful verbs

Often defensible:

- increased or decreased;
- differed in this dataset;
- predicted held-out labels above the stated baseline;
- accounted for a stated fraction of variance;
- was associated with;
- was reproduced by the model under these assumptions.

Often too strong without additional evidence:

- proved;
- caused;
- represented uniquely;
- decoded the thought;
- showed how the brain performs the computation;
- validated the model in general.
